# 42-Piscine-data-science
my solutions to the piscine data science of 42 school

Just a word of advise: on 42 school computers it's very likely you will crash, trust me, I experienced that for queries that ran in 15s on my own pc, my recommendation is to have postgres queries be the ones that separate the data and choose the data you want, instead of relying on pandas too much for that, a bad query is SIGNIFICANTLY slower than a good one
